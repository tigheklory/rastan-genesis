How to use (Phase 1)
  1. Create editable profile (the baseline is read-only).
  2. Pick a track (left). The center-left panel shows its 8 original YM2151 channels with capture-derived activity and confidence.
  3. For each original channel choose a Genesis destination (FM1–5, PSG tone 1–3, PSG noise, or Unassigned). FM6 is a reserved DAC resource and cannot carry music.
  4. A choice starts as a proposal. Click Approve to promote it to an approved decision — proposals and approvals are kept separate.
  5. The Conflicts panel validates your routing live (FM budget, monophonic collisions, tone-3/noise coupling, FM6). Incomplete timing is reported as potential, never proven.
  6. Save writes a versioned JSON profile. Original captures and Lawrence's VGM are never modified.
Audition (playback/A-B) is UNAVAILABLE in Phase 1 — no validated emulator render is wired. The contract exists; no fake oscillator audio is presented as a reference. · AUDIO-003 sequence data is not decoded, so per-note/time-dependent instrument evidence is absent and is marked as such.
