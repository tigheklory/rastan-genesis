=== RETIREMENT / REUSE ===
Individual: 0x427B2 clrb +0x00 when off-screen (+0x24 set, X out of range). Only +0x00 cleared; other fields STALE -> next allocator must fully re-init.
Scene/round: 0x3A67A / 0x3A804 zero entire actor region via 0x3A2D0 fill.
Schedule refill: 0x49F30 tracks slot ownership via +0x26; freeing a slot lets the schedule re-install a new latent actor -> small schedule -> long enemy stream.
